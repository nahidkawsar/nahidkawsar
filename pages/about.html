Who I Am

About Me

I am H.M. Nahid Kawsar, an Applied Mathematics graduate researcher with a strong interest in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and data-driven healthcare solutions. I am currently pursuing an MSc in Applied Mathematics at Noakhali Science and Technology University, where I also serve as a Research Assistant.

My research focuses on designing intelligent and interpretable AI systems for healthcare and mental health applications. I work at the intersection of mathematical modeling, deep learning, and explainable AI, developing solutions for respiratory disease detection, infectious disease modeling, and student mental health risk assessment using the DASS-21 framework.

I have published in peer-reviewed journals, including Biomedical Signal Processing and Control (Q1), Arab Journal of Basic and Applied Sciences (Q2), and presented at international conferences such as IEEE ICCIT, where I received the Best Technical Presentation Award. My work emphasizes methodological rigor, real-world applicability, and model interpretability.

Beyond research, I actively build practical AI systems, including Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) applications, LLM-based chatbots, and NLP-driven tools. I am passionate about transforming theoretical knowledge into deployable solutions that create meaningful and measurable impact.

📐 Department of Applied Mathematics

The Department of Applied Mathematics is one of the most prominent and well-organized departments of Noakhali Science and Technology University (NSTU). In NSTU, this department was inaugurated in 2010.

Dept. of Applied Mathematics offers a 4 years BSc with 160 credit hours and 1 year MSc (Thesis/Non-Thesis) with 34/36 credit hours. In every intake, this department enrolls 50 students under the faculty of science of this university. The 10th batch has already completed their MSc and explored their careers in diversified fields at home and abroad.

The Department of Applied Mathematics is facilitated with adequate faculty members of high academic excellence. Different club activities, lab and class facilities, extra-curricular activities, and supporting staff are also available. If you think to get quality education through a sound environment for a better career, then Dept. of Applied Mathematics is welcoming you.
