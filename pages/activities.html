Enhancing Transfer Learning for Medical Image Classification with SMOTE — Live Presentation
Presenting my paper on using SMOTE to address class imbalance in medical image classification. While alternative methods like ADASYN, Borderline-SMOTE, and GANs exist, they require powerful GPUs and significant training time. This study demonstrates that applying SMOTE on class probabilities extracted from pre-trained CNN models improves accuracy by 1.97%, recall/sensitivity by 5.43%, and specificity by 0.72% — all with minimal extra computation. The Voting Classifier combined with SMOTE achieved 92.14% accuracy on the imbalanced APTOS 2019 diabetic retinopathy dataset.

🤝 NSTU Shine — Bangla Sign Language Program

As Joint Secretary of NSTU Shine (formerly NSTU Society for the Disabled), I played a key role in organizing and successfully executing a Bangla Sign Language awareness program for the university community. From managing mentors and securing administrative permissions to crowd control, arranging meals for participants, and preparing certificates — this was a massive undertaking that required meticulous planning and coordination. The program was completed successfully, making sign language education more accessible to students.

🔥 NSTU Blood Brigade — Fire Safety Program

A 3-day comprehensive fire safety and first aid awareness program organized by NSTU Blood Brigade. We were trained in immediate first aid techniques — including CPR, wound dressing, fracture immobilization, choking management, and burn treatment. Additionally, we learned fire safety protocols such as fire prevention, using different types of fire extinguishers (CO2, dry powder, foam), evacuation procedures, and emergency response coordination. A quiz competition was held based on the training, where I secured 2nd position.

🏃 NSTU Blood Brigade — Cancer Awareness Long Run

A marathon organized by NSTU Blood Brigade with the theme "Run Against Cancer" to raise cancer awareness. While I did not participate in the run itself, I served as a volunteer at hydration points, ensuring participants stayed hydrated throughout the event. Additionally, I provided technical support to the organizers and contributed my management skills to help coordinate the event smoothly.
