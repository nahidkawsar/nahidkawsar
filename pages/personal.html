The Important One

Honorable Family Member

Mizuki
Persian Cat · 3 years old

This is the one being who has never questioned my research methodology or publication strategies. When a paper gets rejected, Mizuki is there. When a deadline passes, Mizuki doesn't judge. Pure, unconditional science companion.

Her weirdest habit? Walking across my keyboard during critical analysis sessions — especially when I'm about to run an important model. Her preferred napping spot is right on top of my printed research papers. 🐾

📚 Books That Shaped My Imagination
Sherlock Holmes
Sir Arthur Conan Doyle (Bengali Translation)
The legendary detective who taught me that every problem has a logical solution. First read during Class 10, and the art of deduction has stayed with me ever since. From BBC's Sherlock to Young Sherlock and RDJ's movies — the brilliance of Holmes continues to inspire.
🔍 Mystery · Detective
গ্রিস ও ট্রয়ের উপাখ্যান
Greece & Troy — Bengali Translation
The epic tales of heroes, gods, and the legendary Trojan War. This book introduced me to the rich mythology of ancient Greece — Achilles, Hector, Odysseus, and the timeless struggle between honor, love, and destiny. A foundation for understanding storytelling itself.
🏛️ Mythology · Epic
White Fang (হোয়াইট ফ্যাং)
Jack London (Bengali Translation)
The journey of a wild wolf-dog from the brutal wilderness to finding loyalty and love. A powerful story about survival, transformation, and the bond between humans and animals. One of the most emotional reads that stayed with me long after finishing.
🐺 Adventure · Survival

My Space

Where It All Happens

My Workspace

This is where the journey happened. From the first day of my BSc, through countless research papers, late-night problem-solving sessions, and the final draft of my honours project — this table witnessed it all.

It's not about the desk or the chair. It's about the coffee cups, the scattered notes, the books stacked higher than my ambitions, and the determination that lived in this small corner. It's also where two novels were born during the quiet days of COVID-19 lockdown — stories of wormholes, time travel, and mysteries waiting to be told.
