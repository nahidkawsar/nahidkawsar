A Computationally Efficient RK4-FNN Hybrid Approach for High-Precision Tuberculosis Dynamics Modeling: SIR/SEIR Model Applications
S. Begum, F. Mahmud, H. M. N. Kawsar, and M. A. Akbar
Arab Journal of Basic and Applied Sciences, Volume 33, Issue 1, pp. 134–151 (2026)
This study introduces a hybrid approach integrating the fourth-order Runge-Kutta method with a Feedforward Neural Network (FNN) for modeling infectious disease dynamics. The tuned FNN achieved relative accuracy of 99.65% for the SIR model and 99.91% for the SEIR model.

M. Z. Alam, T. Roy, H. M. N. Kawsar, and I. Rimi
