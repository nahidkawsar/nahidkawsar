Embedded Machine Learning Framework for Respiratory Disease Detection
M. H. Bijoy, H. M. N. Kawsar, M. T. Islam, K. Hasan, and S. Ahn
Biomedical Signal Processing and Control, Volume 113, Article 109132 (2026)
This study proposes a machine learning-based framework for respiratory disease detection via lung sound analysis, integrated into a real-time embedded system. The ExtraTrees classifier achieved 99.87% accuracy with 10-fold cross-validation on the ICBHI dataset and 93.68% accuracy on the external KAUH dataset.

A Computationally Efficient RK4-FNN Hybrid Approach for High-Precision Tuberculosis Dynamics Modeling: SIR/SEIR Model Applications
S. Begum, F. Mahmud, H. M. N. Kawsar, and M. A. Akbar
Arab Journal of Basic and Applied Sciences, Volume 33, Issue 1, pp. 134–151 (2026)
This study introduces a hybrid approach integrating the fourth-order Runge-Kutta method with a Feedforward Neural Network (FNN) for modeling infectious disease dynamics. The tuned FNN achieved relative accuracy of 99.65% for the SIR model and 99.91% for the SEIR model.

T. Roy, H. M. N. Kawsar, I. Rimi, and M. Z. Alam

Enhancing Transfer Learning for Medical Image Classification with SMOTE: A Comparative Study
M. Z. Alam, T. Roy, H. M. N. Kawsar, and I. Rimi
