A little about me

Biography

Connecting mathematical foundations with practical, responsible AI.

H. M. Nahid Kawsar holds a Bachelor of Science in Applied Mathematics from Noakhali Science and Technology University, Bangladesh, with research interests in machine learning and computer vision. His academic trajectory centres on mathematical optimisation, statistical learning, and numerical methods, bridging applied mathematics and AI.

His work focuses on machine learning, deep learning, and computer vision. He has applied machine learning algorithms, neural networks, ensemble methods, and transfer learning to disease prediction, disease modelling, and medical image classification. His research also explores model performance and lightweight AI for low-resource devices. Through international research collaborations and research assistantships at NSTU, he has gained experience in data analysis and collaborative research.

Building on these experiences, he aims to develop efficient, lightweight machine learning and deep learning models that improve performance, enhance interpretability, and enable deployment on resource-constrained devices. His long-term objective is to contribute to the development of reliable and accessible AI systems through rigorous research.
